The Founders

Two Friends, One Blender

D’ Pleasure was never a business plan, it was two friends who believed a real shake counter belonged in their city. Here’s who’s behind the counter.

Founder

Ritesh Shah

Founder & Head Blender

The one behind the counter at 7am, cutting fruit and testing pours. Ritesh guards the rule the whole counter was built on, if it isn’t blended fresh, it doesn’t leave the bar.

Co Founder

How It Started

From A Sketch To A Splash

Chapter 01

It Started With One Blender

No investors, no playbook, just two friends convinced that a shake made from real fruit and real cream could outsell anything from a packet. The first Oreo Eclipse was blended on a kitchen counter at 2am, and the notebook hasn’t closed since.

batch no. 1, 2:04am
Chapter 02

Recipes Had To Earn Their Place

Every flavor on the menu today survived dozens of test batches, sipped, tweaked, sometimes poured straight down the sink. A recipe only makes the board when both founders agree it’s worth coming back for.

test batch no. 27
Chapter 03

A Dream Project, Open Daily

Today the counter runs on the same rule it started with: real fruit cut that morning, cream poured by hand, nothing pre mixed in the back, and no glass leaves the bar that we wouldn’t drink ourselves.

open day, first pour
